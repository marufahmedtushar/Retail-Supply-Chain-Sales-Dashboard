# Retail-Supply-Chain-Sales-Dashboard

# Objective

Built this dashboard to get a clear picture of retail supply chain sales — tracking revenue, orders, and shipping performance, and figuring out which regions, products, categories, and customers are actually driving the business.

# Sales Overview & Monthly Trend

Insights

• Total Revenue came in at $286.40K across 9.99K orders and 37.87K units, with Total Sales touching $2.30M.

• Sales are all over the place month to month — November hit a high of $34.55K while July dropped all the way down to $10.01K.

• There's a clear pattern of dips followed by strong recoveries (like March into April, and July into August), so the business doesn't stay flat for long.

# Regional Performance

Insights

• West is the clear winner here, pulling in $108.42K, with East close behind at $91.52K.

• South ($46.75K) and Central ($39.71K) are way behind the top two.

• West and East alone make up almost 70% of all regional sales, so a huge chunk of the business is coming from just two regions.

# Shipping & Returns

Insights

• Standard Class is by far the most used shipping option at $164.09K — more than double every other mode combined.

• Second Class ($57.45K) and First Class ($48.97K) get decent use, but Same Day shipping barely gets touched at $15.89K.

• Returns sit at around 8% of total revenue ($23.23K out of $286.40K), which isn't huge but is still worth keeping an eye on.

# Category & Product Analysis

Insights

• Office Supplies leads the categories at roughly $145.45K, with Technology not far behind at $122.49K.

• Furniture is lagging way behind at just $18.45K — might need a pricing or marketing push.

• One product completely dominates on profit: the Canon imageCLASS 2200 series brings in $25.20K, way ahead of the next best (Fellowes PB500 Electric Punch at $7.75K).

• After that top product, profit drops off fast — the business is leaning a lot on one hero product right now.

# Discount Analysis

Insights

• Pressboard Covers with Storage Hooks get discounted the most, at 7.20%.

• Acco D-Ring Binder w/DubLock (6.80%) and Avery Self-Adhesive Photo Pockets (6.40%) aren't far behind.

• Most other discounted products sit in the 5.60%–5.90% range, so discounting looks fairly consistent across binding and office accessory items.

# Customer Analysis

Insights

• Raymond Buch is the top customer, bringing in $8.98K.

• Hunter Lopez ($6.98K) and Tom Ashbrook ($5.76K) come in right after.

• The top 5 customers make up a big chunk of overall customer revenue — the business is fairly dependent on a small group of repeat buyers.

# Overall Conclusion
Overall, this dashboard shows that West and East regions, Standard Class shipping, and the Office Supplies/Technology categories are really carrying the business. That said, there's a heavy reliance on one standout product (Canon imageCLASS 2200) and a small set of top customers, which isn't the most stable position long-term. Returns are manageable at around 8%, but there's clear room to grow Central and Furniture, diversify the product mix a bit, and take a closer look at how discounts are being applied on office accessories.
